💡 Product Insights & Recommendations
ACQUISITION Convert Weekend Casual Riders 27.9% casual share peaks on weekends. Targeted in-app prompts post-ride on Sat/Sun could drive membership conversion — offer a "3-ride trial" to frequent casual users.
OPERATIONS E-Bike Battery Rebalancing 75.1% e-bike adoption means rapid battery depletion at peak stations. Casual riders' 27-min avg trips drain batteries ~2.3× faster. Prioritize charging at top-10 stations during 10am–4pm window.
ENGAGEMENT Member Retention — Morning Commute Lock-in 72.1% of trips are from members with strong 8am/6pm peaks. Product should double down on commuter features: guaranteed bike availability, route memory, and multi-ride passes — high-value engagement moments.
INFRASTRUCTURE Station Capacity Expansion Top 12 stations handle a disproportionate share of starts. Stations near Penn Station, Herald Sq, and Midtown require expanded docking capacity and more frequent rebalancing cycles, especially 7–9am.
